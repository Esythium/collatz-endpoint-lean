VERDICT

The theorem CollatzEndpoint.uniform_window_bound proves the complete mathematical
statement of manuscript Theorem 2.2: the exact exponents beta and gamma, constant
400, every integer window length X >= 2, every positive integer start a, and every
collision-free set. No hidden finiteness assumption on the set, fixed-window
assumption, special-orbit assumption, decimal exponent replacement, or unproved
custom axiom was found. The Lean result is slightly stronger: a may be zero and A
may be any subset of the natural numbers, including zero.

MainResults now supplies the actual Collatz-specific instantiations, including
arbitrary positive even or odd seeds. The complete mathematical scope of the
paper's main theorem, Corollary 5.1, and Corollary 5.2 passes this semantic audit.
There are no remaining assumed counting estimates, recurrence assumptions,
summability assumptions, injectivity assumptions, or odd-seed restrictions in
the final concrete trajectory theorems beyond the paper's own positive-seed and
divergence/growth hypotheses. The exact product identity and convergence to a
finite correction-product limit are also instantiated for the actual odd orbit.

The proof does not show that all positive Collatz trajectories reach 1, exclude
nontrivial cycles, exclude every divergent trajectory, or exclude the logarithmic
boundary c=gamma/beta. Those remain correctly outside the mathematical claims.

1. DEFINITIONS AND DOMAIN

Parity.lean:12 defines step n = if n % 2 = 0 then n / 2 else (3*n+1)/2.
For a positive integer n this is exactly the manuscript shortcut map; division
is exact in each selected parity case. step_pos and orbit_pos prove positivity.
The harmless extension step 0 = 0 is not an extra hypothesis on positive orbits.
Parity.lean:15 defines orbit n k = step^[k] n, so k is the number of shortcut
iterations, not the number of odd iterations.

Counting.lean:12 defines CollisionFree A as
  forall k, Set.InjOn (fun n => orbit n k) A.
This is precisely absence of same-time collisions for distinct members of A.
It does not require distinct members never to share an orbit at different times.
The definition is not vacuous: for example singletons satisfy it, and the full
positive shortcut cycle {1,2} satisfies it. Infinite divergent-orbit value sets
are a later mathematical instantiation, not an assumption inserted here.

Windows.lean:7 defines windowSet A a X by filtering the finite integer interval
Ico a (a+X) by membership in A. The proved membership equivalence is
  a <= n and n < a+X and n in A.
Thus its cardinality is exactly the cardinality printed in the paper. Classical
decidability of set membership causes no restriction on A.

2. EXACT EXPONENTS

Entropy.lean:90 selects alpha from a proved existence statement, not an axiom.
The proof establishes 3/5 < alpha < 5/8 and
  Real.binEntropy alpha = alpha * Real.log 3.
alpha_unique establishes uniqueness throughout [1/2,1]. Real.binEntropy uses
natural logarithms. On (0,1) its definition is
  -p*ln p -(1-p)*ln(1-p).
Division by ln 2 gives the paper's H_2, so this is the same entropy crossing.

The definitions at lines 117--123 are
  beta = alpha*ln 3/ln 2,
  entropySlope = ln(1-alpha)-ln alpha,
  entropyGap = ln 3-entropySlope,
  gamma = ln 3/(2*entropyGap).
Since H_2'(alpha)=entropySlope/ln 2 and lambda=ln 3/ln 2,
these are exactly beta=lambda*alpha and gamma=lambda/(2*(lambda-H_2'(alpha))).
No decimal value is used as a definition or hypothesis.

3. FINITE COUNTING AND ANALYSIS CHAIN

The Lean aligned blocks are [q*2^k,(q+1)*2^k), whereas the paper presents the
equally valid representatives q*2^k+{1,...,2^k}. This is an intentional change of
residue representatives, not an endpoint loss. dyadic_translation is proved for
all natural representatives, including zero. representative_orbit_lt proves the
needed endpoint upper bound also for the zero representative.

For a fixed odd-count layer s, parity-word injectivity gives at most choose(k,s)
members. The translated endpoint interval is [q*3^s,(q+2)*3^s), whose integer
cardinality is exactly 2*3^s. CollisionFree supplies injectivity of the k-th
endpoint on this layer. Finite fiber decomposition sums these independent
minimum bounds over s=0,...,k. Counting.lean:24 therefore defines precisely
  layerSum k = sum_{s=0}^k min(choose(k,s),2*3^s).

LayerEstimate.lean:441 proves, for every natural k with 1 <= k,
  (layerSum k : Real) <= 100*2^(beta*k)*k^(-gamma).
The natural floor cutoff is positive in the large-k branch, so using Nat.floor
does not accidentally truncate a negative real cutoff. Its two strict ratio
bounds are established for all k >= 64. The small-k branch covers all 1 <= k <= 63.
The formal proof uses mathlib's global Stirling inequalities to obtain the same
coarse binomial prefactor 2; it need not reproduce Robbins' sharper intermediate
formula from the prose. This proof-route difference leaves the stated result
and explicit constants unchanged.

WindowCover chooses k=Nat.clog 2 X and proves
  1 <= k, X <= 2^k, 2^k < 2*X
for every X >= 2. Every half-open window of length X lies in at most two of the
aligned blocks. The cardinality inequality is obtained for the actual filtered
window, not for a different translate. WindowEstimate uses beta in [0,1] and
gamma >= 0 to convert 200*2^(beta*k)*k^(-gamma) to
400*X^beta/(log_2 X)^gamma. All logarithm bases and positivity requirements are
checked; log_2 X is positive when X >= 2.

5. GROWTH AND RECIPROCAL LEMMAS

OrbitGrowth.prefixMax z N is sup over indices 0,...,N-1, exactly the manuscript
prefix maximum. Its use is always at N >= 2, so the empty-set default is irrelevant.
Positive injective sequences satisfy N <= prefixMax z N, proved by interval
cardinality, without a monotonicity assumption on z.

InitialCountingBound quantifies over all finite subsets of A lying in [1,X].
It follows from uniform_window_bound with a=1 and window length X, since
Ico 1 (1+X) consists exactly of the positive natural numbers <= X.
prefixMax_lower_bound algebraically gives the exact constant C^(-1/beta) and
logarithmic exponent gamma/beta. Setting C=400 gives the displayed Corollary 5.1
bound. The exponents in the inversion have their correct signs and parentheses.

not_isBigO_of_prefixMax_lower excludes every real c<r, including c<0. For negative
c its proof first replaces c by max(c,0), which remains <r because r>0. It then
uses a comparison scale monotone from index 2 onwards. This is a valid weakening
for contradiction; it does not discard the negative-c cases or assume the
trajectory itself is monotone.

Reciprocal.reciprocal_summable_of_power_count assumes an injective positive
enumeration contained in A and a power counting bound C*X^beta with beta<1.
The hypotheses match the intended use after discarding the beneficial logarithm
denominator (log_2 X)^gamma >= 1 for X>=2. Its dyadic shell decomposition is over
arbitrary finite sets of indices, which correctly proves Summable for an
unordered/injective trajectory. The shell index r=0 includes the possible value
1, so there is no omitted low-value term. No monotone enumeration is assumed.
The geometric ratio is 2^(beta-1)<1, exactly the necessary power saving.

6. FINAL CONCRETE TRAJECTORY THEOREMS: ALL BRIDGES DISCHARGED

MainResults.initial_counting_bound derives the full counting bound from the
window theorem with a=1. initial_power_counting_bound weakens it using gamma>0
and log_2 X>=1. These are proofs, not final theorem assumptions.

Dynamics.Divergent start is Tendsto (orbit start) atTop atTop. This definition
matches the manuscript's infinite-value-set convention: repeated values force
a periodic tail, while an injective sequence of natural numbers leaves every
finite initial interval. Dynamics proves both
  Divergent start <-> not EventuallyPeriodic start,
  Divergent start <-> Function.Injective (orbit start).
A separate Lean example was compiled to check explicitly that the manuscript
hypothesis (Set.range (orbit start)).Infinite implies Divergent start using the
proved generic injective_iterates_of_infinite_range. Thus this choice of
definition does not silently strengthen the manuscript's hypothesis.

EventuallyPeriodic start is exactly
  exists i p : Nat, 0<p and forall n>=i, orbit start (n+p)=orbit start n.
It allows a preperiod and requires a positive period. It does not mean reaching
1, and it does not exclude a nontrivial cycle. The formal dynamical alternative
eventuallyPeriodic_or_divergent is valid for this deterministic map on Nat.

Dynamics.collisionFree_of_divergent uses the actual full shortcut orbit value
set. It proves that a same-time collision of orbit values would yield equal
indices i+k=j+k, hence i=j, by injectivity. The supplied collision-free set is
therefore not an unrelated or restricted subset.

Drift.firstOdd n = n / 2^(padicValNat 2 n). For every positive n,
firstOdd_eq_orbit identifies this with the actual shortcut iterate at time
padicValNat 2 n, and firstOdd_pos and firstOdd_mod_two establish a positive odd
value. This removes only the initial even segment. successiveOddOrbit n k is
oddOrbit (firstOdd n) k, so even initial seeds are covered.

oddValuation y = padicValNat 2 (3*y+1), and oddStep y divides 3*y+1 by precisely
this power of two. Its product identity is exact. For odd y the valuation is
positive, and oddStep_eq_orbit identifies the transition with exactly that many
shortcut steps (one odd shortcut step and all remaining halvings).
successiveOddTimes is strictly increasing, and successiveOddOrbit_eq_orbit
identifies each enumerated odd value with an actual original shortcut iterate.
The positivity, range inclusion and injectivity arguments are fully supplied.
There is no leftover assumption that an arbitrary sequence behaves like an odd
Collatz trajectory.

Corollary 5.1 is covered by:
  divergent_prefixMax_lower             (shortcut, every N>=2),
  divergent_odd_prefixMax_lower         (successive odd, every N>=2),
  divergent_not_isBigO                  (pointwise shortcut sequence),
  divergent_odd_not_isBigO              (pointwise odd sequence),
  eventuallyPeriodic_of_critical_power  (arbitrary positive seed),
  eventuallyPeriodic_of_odd_critical_power (arbitrary positive seed).
The two lower bounds have the exact constant 400^(-1/beta) and exact exponent
gamma/beta. The excluded Big-O bounds are statements about z_n itself, not
merely prefixMax z N. The general lemmas transfer an eventual pointwise bound
to prefix maxima with a changed multiplicative constant and account for the
finite initial segment. All real c<gamma/beta are covered. The more general
eventuallyPeriodic_of_isBigO and eventuallyPeriodic_of_odd_isBigO are stronger
than the paper's pure-power final sentence and are consistent with it.

7. PRODUCT, DRIFT, LIMSUP, AND EXTENDED-REAL COERCIONS

collatzDrift start n is exactly
  n*log_2 3 - sum_{j=0}^{n-1} padicValNat 2 (3*successiveOddOrbit start j+1).
The sum is a Nat sum cast to Real after summing, so negative drift values remain
possible; this is not truncated natural subtraction.

collatz_product_identity proves for every positive seed and every n the exact
identity y_n=y_0*2^(collatzDrift start n)*correctionProduct y n.
correctionProduct is the finite product of 1+1/(3*y_j), with Q_0=1.
The divergent shortcut and odd reciprocal-summability theorems supply the
previous generic counting hypotheses internally. The odd summability theorem
also supplies Summable (1/(3*y_j)) by a constant multiple.

correctionProduct_mono, one_le_correctionProduct and its exponential upper bound
are proved. divergent_correctionProduct_converges supplies an actual finite real
Q>=1, bounded above by exp(sum 1/(3*y_j)), and Tendsto Q_n (nhds Q).
A Lean example independently instantiated the generic convergence lemma to the
actual successiveOddOrbit, and the final named concrete theorem was then read
and axiom-audited. No convergence assumption is hidden in the final result.

Corollary 5.2 is covered by divergent_not_drift_upper, which quantifies over
every real B and every real c<gamma/beta and denies the corresponding eventual
upper inequality. This is precisely the stated +O(1) exclusion, since such an
upper O(1) remainder supplies one finite constant B. The case c=0 is included.
The exact drift identity and finite correction-product bound convert the
eventual drift envelope to a pointwise orbit Big-O bound, which is then excluded.

divergent_drift_frequently_gt proves that for each c<gamma/beta the normalized
drift exceeds c infinitely often. Its proof restricts to n>=3 when multiplying
by log_2(log_2 n), thereby ensuring strict positivity of the denominator.
Lean's total values at n=0,1,2 do not affect the atTop result.

divergent_drift_limsup takes Filter.limsup in EReal. It permits +infinity without
an additional bounded-above hypothesis and gives the mathematically intended
extended-real limsup inequality. The source spelling (gamma/beta : EReal) was
explicitly checked by a compiled rfl example to be the same as coercing the
real quotient ((gamma/beta : Real) : EReal). Thus there is no accidental switch
to a different quotient or a failure caused by zero/infinite operands. beta>0
has independently been proved. The generic limsup proof also transports the
comparison c<gamma/beta using order-preserving real-to-EReal coercions.
